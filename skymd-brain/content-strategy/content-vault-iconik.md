# SkyMD AI Content Vault & B-Roll Management System

*Meeting with Stefan — June 10, 2026*

## Objective

Build a searchable AI-powered content library that allows the content team to instantly locate relevant footage without digging through folders. Maximize the value of 18+ months of existing content, reduce editing time, and increase content variety.

---

## Current Challenge

Over a year and a half of content spread across multiple hard drives and folders.

**Current workflow:**
- Search through folders manually
- Try to remember where clips were filmed
- Reuse the same B-roll repeatedly
- Spend excessive time locating footage

**This creates:** slower editing, less output, repetitive visuals, underutilized library.

---

## Vision

Instead of searching folders, editors search concepts.

| Search | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Sauna | All sauna clips across entire library |
| Stefan | All Stefan footage |
| Cold Plunge | All cold plunge clips |
| First Responder | All first responder footage |
| John Peel | All John footage |

---

## Recommended Software

**Primary: Iconik**

Why Iconik:
- AI-powered tagging
- Facial recognition
- Speech-to-text transcription
- Google Drive + local hard drive integration
- Searchable video library
- Scalable

**Alternatives:** Mimir, Adobe Premiere Pro (limited), Axle AI

---

## Rollout Plan

### Phase 1: Library Assessment (Week 1)

Inventory all media sources: SanDisk SSDs, external hard drives, Google Drive, Dropbox.

Identify total storage size, number of files, highest-value footage.

**Master folder categories:**

- **People** — Stefan, John Peel, Doctors, Athletes, First Responders
- **Activities** — Running, Gym, Recovery, Sauna, Cold Plunge, Stretching
- **Product** — Product shots, mixing packets, drinking product, lifestyle hydration
- **Locations** — Gym, Office, Travel, Medical, Events

### Phase 2: AI Indexing (Weeks 2–4)

Do NOT manually tag content. Connect library to Iconik and let AI generate:
- Face recognition
- Speech transcription
- Object detection
- Activity recognition
- Keyword generation

Example auto-tags on a single clip: `Stefan Wolf`, `Sauna`, `Hydration`, `Recovery`, `Wellness`, `Talking`

### Phase 3: Build Hero Collections

| Collection | Contents |
|------------|----------|
| Stefan | All Stefan footage |
| John Peel | All John footage |
| Running | All running footage |
| Sauna | All sauna footage |
| Recovery | Sauna, stretching, cold plunge, mobility |
| Product | All product footage |
| First Responder | Firefighters, police, military |
| Doctor | Medical experts, educational content |
| HYROX | Training and competition footage |
| Lifestyle | Travel, work, daily habits |

### Phase 4: Build Content Packs

Pre-built B-roll packs for rapid editing:

- **Hydration Pack** — Drinking water, mixing packets, product shots, electrolyte visuals
- **Performance Pack** — Running, training, strength work, sprinting
- **Recovery Pack** — Sauna, cold plunge, stretching, mobility
- **Lifestyle Pack** — Travel, office, family, daily routines
- **Medical Pack** — Doctors, clinics, educational visuals
- **First Responder Pack** — Firefighters, police, tactical training

---

## B-Roll Rotation System

Track times used and last used date per clip.

**Priority order:**
1. Never-used clips
2. Least-used clips
3. Frequently-used clips

Ensures content stays visually fresh.

---

## Future Editing Workflow

**Target: under 10 minutes to source all B-roll.**

1. Write hook or text overlay (e.g. *"Most people are dehydrated before 10 AM."*)
2. Search relevant collections (office, travel, gym, hydration, running)
3. Pull 5–10 clips from multiple collections
4. Add text overlay
5. Export

---

## Success Metrics (90 Days)

- Reduce B-roll sourcing time by 75%
- Increase variety of clips used
- Reduce repeated footage
- Create searchable archive of all footage
- Build reusable content packs
- Increase content output without increasing filming requirements

## Long-Term Goal: The SkyMD Content Vault

Anyone on the team types `"Stefan sauna"`, `"doctor hydration"`, `"cold plunge"`, `"electrolytes"` — and instantly retrieves every relevant clip from the entire library.

More content from footage we already own. Less dependence on constantly filming new B-roll.
