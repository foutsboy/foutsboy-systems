## The 2-Week Cycle (Full Flow)

1. **Week 1 + 2:** Shoot across the week's natural locations — always Saguaro Track, plus whatever else is in rotation
2. **Luke:** Edits short-form as normal, holds clips (does not post)
3. **Luke:** Builds content log summary after each session
4. **End of Week 2:** All footage + content log → ADAR for first draft long-form cut
5. **Luke reviews:** One pass of notes
6. **Pavin + Alfie QC:** 30–60 day window
7. **Stefan approves:** Final sign-off only
8. **YouTube publishes first**
9. **Short-form rolls out** over 1–2 weeks after — drives back to full video

---

## Why We Keep Repeating Conversations

Inconsistent shooting means we haven't built the muscle yet. When we go 3 weeks without a shoot, we restart the strategy conversation because nothing feels locked in.

The framework above is only real when it runs. Two cycles of execution will matter more than any planning conversation.
