# Stef's Long-Form to Short-Form SOP (v1)

*Work in progress — expect this to evolve as the system matures.*

---

## The Core Idea

Luke's short-form edits throughout the week ARE the selects. Every clip he cuts points the editor to the best moment in the raw footage. Nothing gets timestamped manually — the edit is the timestamp.

Short-form and long-form are not two separate workflows. They're one pipeline.

---

## Cycle Length

**Two weeks per YouTube.** Content log tracks everything shot during the cycle.

---

## Roles

| Person | Role |
|--------|------|
| Luke | Primary editor (short-form), producer, content log owner |
| Editor (ADAR) | YouTube assembly, long-form rough cut |
| Stefan | Shoots, approves final cuts only |

---

## The Weekly Workflow

### During the Two Weeks — Luke

1. **Shoot and log** — content log stays updated with every session (date, location, what was captured). No timestamps needed.
2. **Edit short-form as normal** — cut the clips you'd normally post. One per session or however many make sense.
3. **Do not post them yet.** Hold every clip.

That's it. Luke's cuts are the treasure map for the editor.

### End of Week Two — Editor

1. Takes Luke's short-form cuts and the content log
2. Uses each cut to locate the key moment in the raw footage
3. Pulls those moments, extends them with context, adds B-roll and connecting tissue
4. Assembles YouTube rough cut
5. Delivers to Luke for review

### Luke Reviews

- One pass of notes
- Editor tightens
- Stefan approves

---

## Publish Order

1. **YouTube publishes first**
2. **Short-form clips roll out over 1-2 weeks after** — driving viewers back to the full video

Short-form is a trailer for the YouTube, not a duplicate. The viewer sees a 45-second clip, wants more, watches the full video.

---

## Content Log

Stored in: `skymd-brain/content-strategy/stefs-youtube/`

One file per two-week cycle. Tracks:
- Date and location of each shoot
- What was captured
- Who was in it
- Any notable moments called out in the moment

No timestamps required — Luke's short-form edits handle that.
